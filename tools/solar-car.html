Solar Car STEM Challenge

A Year 7 project that joins Science, Maths and Technology around a model solar car that logs its own data.

Designed and built independently, outside my school roles. Demonstrations use made-up data and sample AI responses.

The classroom problem

Cross-curricular projects often feel like three separate subjects. Here the same car and the same data carry students from a science question to a maths analysis to an engineering redesign.

What it does

  • Discover: students write a hypothesis and log runs with a micro:bit on the car.
  • Analyse: charts and statistics from their own runs.
  • Engineer: a design brief to improve the car.
  • A teacher view of every group's results.

How it was built

There is no AI inside the site. I designed it and built it with AI coding assistants.

The teacher’s role: Use the teacher view to follow each group’s results across the investigation.
