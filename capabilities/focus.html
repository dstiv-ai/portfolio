Why it matters for learning

Attention is a condition for learning. Clear task boundaries help a class work with purpose and give the teacher a way to pause, explain and refocus.

How I build it

  • I put task access in the teacher’s hands, with a visible state for students.
  • I distinguish a classroom workflow from a security guarantee. These demos simulate access controls in one browser; they do not lock a device or prove authorship.

Open, pause and lock a classroom

The teacher opens a writing task, pauses work for instruction, resumes it or locks it. Student preview makes the effect of that decision visible.

Teacher control. The teacher chooses when writing is available. The demo illustrates the access workflow, without enforcing security across real devices.

Pause an investigation

Open lesson, Pause lesson and Close lesson give an extended investigation a clear rhythm. A submitted draft becomes read-only until the next lesson or a reset.

Teacher control. The teacher controls lesson access and when the investigation moves on. This is a local simulation, not a browser lock.
