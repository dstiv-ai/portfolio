Why it matters for learning

Practice is more useful when a learner can retrieve an idea, see why an answer works and choose what to revisit. Variation and worked examples support that cycle.

How I build it

  • I make the scope of each question bank clear and separate practice feedback from formal assessment.
  • The teacher selects the learning focus and judges readiness. A diagnostic gap is a prompt for teaching, not a fixed label for a learner.
