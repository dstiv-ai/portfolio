Why it matters for learning

Extended inquiry needs continuity. Keeping sources beside a developing explanation lets students return to a claim, weigh the evidence and improve their reasoning.

How I build it

  • I design for a sequence of lessons, not just a single submission.
  • The teacher controls the lesson stage. Saving in these demos is browser-local, with no account or cross-device synchronisation.
