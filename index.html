Approach

From a classroom question to a next step.

Context

A classroom task comes first: a question to argue, an investigation to continue, practice to choose, or equipment to share.

Experience

Learners write, test, design or retrieve an idea, and that work stays where it can be looked at.

Reflection

Criteria, evidence and a next step sit beside the response, so feedback has something to point to.

Action

The teacher opens the task, pauses it, approves feedback or chooses what the class needs next.
